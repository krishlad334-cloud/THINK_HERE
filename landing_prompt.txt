# THINK – COMPLETE SCROLL-BASED ANIMATED LANDING PAGE

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

I want to transform the existing THINK website landing page into a premium, immersive, scroll-based storytelling experience.

The landing page must communicate the complete journey of a person who is struggling with a problem, searching for answers across different AI platforms and websites, discovering THINK, connecting with a real human consultant, receiving personalized guidance, applying that advice, and finally achieving success.

I want to use the SECOND ANIMATION CONCEPT (Searching Everywhere) as the primary visual direction and animation reference for the entire landing page.

Specifically, I want the human character interacting with multiple floating browser windows, search interfaces, AI chat responses, and online platforms.

Extend this visual language throughout all five scenes while keeping the same human character, consistent animation style, and storytelling environment.

This should feel like a premium animated product experience, not a collection of static sections.

## 2. CORE STORYLINE

The entire landing page must contain five connected scroll-based scenes:

1. OVERTHINKING – A person struggles with a problem.
2. SEARCHING EVERYWHERE – The person searches AI tools and online platforms but cannot find sufficiently specific guidance.
3. DISCOVERING THINK – The person discovers the THINK platform.
4. HUMAN CONNECTION – The person connects with a consultant and receives personalized advice.
5. APPLY & SUCCEED – The person applies the advice and achieves meaningful progress.

The emotional journey:

Confusion → Information Overload → Discovery → Human Guidance → Success.

Main brand message:

"AI Can Give You Answers. THINK Connects You With The Right Human Expertise."

# 5. SCENE 02 – SEARCHING EVERYWHERE (PRIMARY ANIMATION REFERENCE)

IMPORTANT: This is the most visually important reference scene.

Use the second animation concept as the main visual inspiration for this section.

I specifically want a human character interacting with multiple browser windows, AI interfaces, websites, and search results.

Do not make this a simple laptop animation. Create a visually rich, cinematic information-overload experience.

### Story

The person starts searching for solutions using different AI platforms, search engines, websites, and online resources.

Initially, they feel hopeful because they receive many answers.

However, most responses are general suggestions that do not fully address their unique circumstances.

The person continues searching but still cannot identify the right solution for their specific problem.

IMPORTANT: Do not suggest that AI platforms are useless. Show that they are helpful for general information, but personalized human expertise can provide a different level of understanding.

### Detailed Animation Sequence

**Animation A – Opening Multiple Platforms**

- The person starts typing on their laptop.
- A search window appears.
- Additional browser windows emerge around the laptop.
- Multiple AI chat interfaces appear.
- Search engine results appear as separate floating panels.
- Different websites and information cards become visible.

**Animation B – AI and Search Results**

Show multiple animated interface cards with sample responses.

Examples:

"Here are 10 ways to solve your problem."

"Try these general strategies."

"Follow these recommended steps."

"Here are some useful resources."

Use different visual layouts for search results and AI responses rather than repeating identical cards.

**Animation C – Information Overload**

- More browser windows appear.
- Windows move into different depth layers.
- Some cards slide from left to right.
- Other cards appear from behind the laptop.
- Search results overlap progressively.
- AI chat responses animate line by line.
- The character moves their eyes between different screens.
- The character looks increasingly confused.

**Animation D – The Missing Answer**

Eventually, the person stops scrolling and looks at the different responses.

Show a prominent thought bubble:

"But which solution is right for MY situation?"

The person realizes that receiving more information does not necessarily mean finding the right direction.

**Animation E – Transition to THINK**

- The browser windows gradually stop moving.
- Unnecessary information cards fade away.
- The camera focuses on the main character.
- A clean transition begins.
- The THINK website becomes the focal point in the next scene.

### Scroll-Based Behaviour

Use scroll progress to control:
- Browser window appearance.
- AI response card animations.
- Search results movement.
- Character expressions.
- Camera zoom.
- Layered parallax.
- Information-overload effect.
- Final transition.

The animation must respond naturally to scrolling rather than playing as an unrelated automatic video.

### Website Text

Heading:

"More Answers. Still No Clear Direction."

Description:

"You can search everywhere, but finding advice that truly fits your situation is different."
